**Supplementary Fig. 3 | Arrival of new instrument models by continent.** **a,** Africa's 6-year lag in new instrument models is about what its 69 datasets predict. **Supplementary Fig. 5 | Software and sequence-database age.** **a,** When a dataset goes public, its Mascot release is typically ≥5.2 years old; its Skyline release ≥1.4. Sensitivity: anchoring each release at the second contributor group to record it gives medians Skyline 1.2, FragPipe 1.5, DIA-NN 2.1, MaxQuant 3.3, Mascot 4.9 y. Excluded: 2 datasets whose PRIDE release date precedes the first-seen submission; 4 Skyline files whose "version" was "64-bit". **b–e,** Deposited sequence databases are typically 1.7 years old, and 42% are older than two years. PRIDE datasets released 2013–2025. b, c: age of the main FASTA at submission, from a high-confidence date in its file name (2,558 datasets, 1,305 groups; median 1.67 y, 95% CI 1.58–1.80; >1 y 69%, >5 y 8%). A name date may be a download, release or tool build date, so ages are approximate.
